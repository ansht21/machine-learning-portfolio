# Retention Policy v3.2

## Offer eligibility
- Rate Waiver: approved for customers on month-to-month contracts >= 3 months tenure with electronic-check payment. Standard discount = 10% of monthly charge, cap $25/month.
- Technical Audit: mandatory for fiber-optic customers who opened >= 2 support tickets in 90 days. Cost = technician slot at $30.
- Plan Upgrade: new-tenure customers (0-6 months) on single-service plans; offer one free premium add-on for 3 months (cost ~$8/month).
- Competitor Price Match: only with a documented competitor quote; match up to 5% of monthly charge.

## Approval matrix
- Offers <= $15/mo: retention analyst auto-approved.
- $15-25/mo: senior analyst sign-off.
- > $25/mo: manager sign-off.

## Response SLA
- High-risk churn score: contact within 24 hours.
- Medium risk: within 72 hours.
- Document every offer in CRM with the retrieved evidence used.

## Escalation
If a customer cites a billing dispute in their complaint, do NOT issue a Rate Waiver;
raise a billing-ticket instead. Never offer two incentives in one contact.
